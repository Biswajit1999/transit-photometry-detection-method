# Image Sourcing — Transit Photometry

Guidance for adding illustrative images to this repo. Two tracks: real
archival imagery first, AI-generation as a fallback. Every image needs
a caption naming the real mission/instrument depicted — AI-generated
images must say so explicitly and must never name the generating tool.

## Real sources to check first

- NASA/JPL "Exoplanet Exploration" image library (public domain) —
  <https://exoplanets.nasa.gov/resources/>
- Kepler and TESS mission press-image galleries —
  <https://images.nasa.gov/> (search "Kepler" or "TESS")
- ESA/Kepler animations and stills of a transit light curve

**hero:** search "Kepler transit light curve animation still" or
"TESS planet transit diagram NASA" on images.nasa.gov.

**instrument:** an official Kepler or TESS spacecraft photo from
images.nasa.gov (public domain).

## AI-generation fallback prompts

- **hero:** "Wide artist's concept of a dark exoplanet silhouette
  crossing the face of a bright yellow Sun-like star, dramatic
  dimming visible, deep space background, photorealistic space art"
- **3d:** "Close-up 3D render of a rocky exoplanet mid-transit,
  limb-darkened stellar disk behind it, side lighting showing the
  ingress edge"
- **instrument:** "Photorealistic illustration of the Kepler space
  telescope in orbit, gold sunshield, Earth in background" (swap for
  TESS if that's the more relevant mission for the surrounding text)

## Citation rule

- AI-generated: caption states "AI-generated illustration/concept —
  not a real photograph," names the real mission/instrument, never
  names the generating tool.
- Real archival photo: cite the actual mission/observatory directly —
  no AI-credit line needed.
